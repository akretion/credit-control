Provider agnostic credit rating of companies.

Ratings are stored in a dedicated ``credit.rating`` model linked to the
commercial partner (no extra column on ``res_partner``): normalized
score from 0 to 100, risk level and the company reference at the
provider. Provides a search wizard to identify the company (name, tax
ID, company ID, country) and a scheduled action to refresh monitored
companies.

Providers are implemented in separate modules, e.g.
``partner_credit_rating_allianz``. Use the ``_rating_changed()`` hook of
``credit.rating`` to react to a rating change (sale blocking, payment
terms...).

Configuration
=============

Choose the provider in *Settings > General Settings > Contacts > Credit
Rating*.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

This module stores one rating per company. Several providers per company
can be added by an extra module, e.g. ``partner_credit_rating_multi``,
without changing this one:

- ``credit.rating``: redefine ``_partner_uniq`` with the same name as
  ``unique(partner_id, provider)``.
- ``res.partner._get_credit_rating(provider)``: do not reuse the rating
  of another provider, always create a new one.
- ``res.partner._get_main_credit_rating()``: return the rating with the
  worst risk (then the most recent), so that ``credit_risk`` shows the
  worst one.
- ``res.partner.credit_risk``: make it computed with an exact search
  method on the worst risk (the default search matches any rating of the
  company).
- Search wizard: show the ``provider`` field (hidden here).
- Partner form: show the number of ratings and open the list when
  several.
- Settings: the company provider becomes the default provider of the
  wizard.
